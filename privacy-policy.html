Privacy Policy

Effective Date: August 27, 2026

Crestline Tax & Accounting ("Crestline Tax & Accounting," "we," "us," or "our") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting the personal information you provide through our website, crestlinetax.com (the "Website").

This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, how we may share it, and the choices you may have regarding your information.

By using our Website or submitting information through our forms, you acknowledge the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

1. Information We Collect

Depending on how you interact with our Website and services, we may collect the following categories of information:

Information You Provide Directly

You may voluntarily provide information when you:

  • Contact us through a contact form
  • Request a consultation or appointment
  • Request information about our services
  • Subscribe to newsletters, updates, or other communications
  • Communicate with us by email or telephone
  • Engage us for tax preparation, tax planning, accounting, bookkeeping, or related services

This information may include:

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Telephone number
  • Mailing or business address
  • Business information
  • Information about the services you are interested in
  • Other information you choose to provide

Tax and Financial Information

If you become a client, we may collect information necessary to provide tax, accounting, bookkeeping, business advisory, or related professional services.

Depending on the services you request, this may include financial, tax, business, identification, income, expense, and other information necessary to perform the requested services.

We only request information that is reasonably necessary for the services being provided.

2. Information Collected Automatically

When you visit our Website, certain information may be collected automatically by your browser, hosting provider, analytics services, or other technology.

This may include:

  • IP address
  • Browser type
  • Device type
  • Operating system
  • Pages visited
  • Approximate location
  • Referring website
  • Date and time of visits
  • Website usage and interaction information

We may use cookies, pixels, analytics tools, or similar technologies to help operate, secure, analyze, and improve our Website.

3. How We Use Your Information

We may use the information we collect to:

  • Respond to inquiries and requests
  • Schedule and manage consultations or appointments
  • Provide tax, accounting, bookkeeping, and related services
  • Communicate with clients and prospective clients
  • Provide requested information
  • Send newsletters or other communications when you have requested or consented to receive them
  • Improve our Website and services
  • Understand how visitors use our Website
  • Maintain website security
  • Prevent fraud, misuse, or unauthorized activity
  • Comply with applicable legal and regulatory requirements
  • Maintain appropriate business and professional records
  • Fulfill other purposes disclosed at the time information is collected

4. Newsletter and Marketing Communications

If you voluntarily subscribe to our newsletter or marketing communications, we may use your name and email address to send you information about tax planning, accounting, business services, educational content, company updates, or other relevant information.

You may unsubscribe from marketing emails at any time by using the unsubscribe link included in the communication or by contacting us directly.

Unsubscribing from marketing communications does not necessarily stop service-related or transactional communications that are necessary to provide services to you.

5. Cookies and Similar Technologies

Our Website may use cookies and similar technologies.

Cookies may help us:

  • Keep the Website functioning properly
  • Remember certain preferences
  • Understand Website traffic and usage
  • Improve Website performance
  • Measure the effectiveness of our communications and marketing

You may be able to control cookies through your browser settings. Disabling certain cookies may affect the functionality of portions of the Website.

6. Third-Party Services

We may use third-party service providers to help operate our Website and business.

These providers may include services for:

  • Website hosting
  • Appointment scheduling
  • Email communications
  • Analytics
  • Marketing
  • Form processing
  • Payment processing
  • Customer relationship management
  • Security and fraud prevention

These third parties may have access to information only as reasonably necessary to perform services on our behalf and may process information according to their own privacy policies.

7. How We Share Information

We do not sell or rent your personal information as a business practice.

We may share information with:

  • Service providers assisting us with Website or business operations
  • Professional advisers, where appropriate
  • Government authorities or regulatory agencies when required by law
  • Courts or other parties when legally required
  • Parties involved in a business transaction, such as a merger, acquisition, financing, restructuring, or sale of assets
  • Other parties when necessary to protect our rights, property, users, or the security of our Website

We may also share information when you specifically authorize us to do so.

8. Protection of Tax and Financial Information

Because tax and accounting services may involve sensitive financial information, we take reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational measures designed to protect information against unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, or destruction.

However, no electronic transmission or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

You should not submit highly sensitive information through an unsecured Website form unless specifically instructed to do so.

We retain personal information for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this Privacy Policy, including providing services, maintaining business records, resolving disputes, enforcing agreements, and satisfying legal, tax, accounting, or regulatory obligations.

Retention periods may vary depending on the type of information and the reason it was collected.

Depending on applicable law, you may have rights regarding your personal information, which may include the ability to:

To make a privacy-related request, contact us using the information below.
